|入口|自动字段|原公式|
|---|---|---|
|GrayStockAdjustDetail、ProdStockAdjustDetail、SemiStockAdjustDetail|AdjustLengthQty|ISNULL(RealLengthQty,0) − ISNULL(PaperLength,0)|
